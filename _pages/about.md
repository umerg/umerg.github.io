I work on **generative models for scientific applications** - how to make them fast and controllable, how to build in structure from the domain (geometry, topology, symmetry), and what their internal representations can tell us about the science itself.

My current research, with the University of Leipzig and ETH Zurich, develops generative methods for branching structures in nature, such as neurons and botanical trees. The first of this line of work appeared at the [GRaM Workshop](https://gram-workshop.github.io) at ICLR 2026; a [follow-up](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38506) is under review at ICLR 2027.

Alongside research, I lead ML at New Gradient and founded Calterra, a spin-off from one of our projects. My [applied work](/projects/) spans subsurface geology, protein synthesis and ecosystem monitoring.

Before that, I was a Research Associate at 3ie, designing controlled trials and multi-country evidence syntheses for policy interventions. I hold an MSc in Data Science from the University of Edinburgh (Distinction, 2023), where I wrote my dissertation with Amazon on recommender systems, and a BSc (Hons) in Mathematics from Sri Venkateswara College, University of Delhi. I am applying to PhD programmes for Fall 2027.
